## 👨🏻‍💻 Why this project exists? 

In this day and age, many people find it difficult to recognize their true selves. Psychological tests can be quite expensive for identifying a person's individual identity. Therefore, the MBTI Test is here to provide a self-assessment with 16 different personality categories that anyone can try. By answering 40 questions, you can discover who you truly are.


## ֎🇦🇮 Project Introduction

The MBTI test is built using a simple C++ programming language, spanning around 8,000 lines of code. This test script is designed to provide you with a deeper understanding of who you truly are.

Without the need for an internet connection or high costs, this MBTI test can be easily used to discover your current personality type.

Supporting both Indonesian and English, the MBTI Test is highly accessible and perfectly adapted for a global audience.

## 👤 16 Personality 
- INTJ (Architect) :
  Strategic, logical, and deeply analytical thinkers who love perfecting details and creating long-term plans.
- INTP (Logician) :
  Innovative, flexible, and intensely curious philosophers who love analyzing data, patterns, and abstract  theories.
- ENTJ (Commander) :
  Decisive, bold, and natural-born leaders who excel at organizing people, systems, and strategic goals.
- ENTP (Debater) :
  Smart, curious, and witty thinkers who love intellectual challenges, playing devil's advocate, and exploring new ideas.
- INFJ (Advocate) :
  Quiet, mystical, yet deeply inspiring idealists guided by strong morals and a desire to help others deeply.
- INFP (Mediator) :
  Poetic, kind, and altruistic individuals who are always eager to help a good cause and stay true to their inner values.
- ENFJ (Protagonist) :
  Charismatic, passionate, and inspiring leaders who easily connect with others and drive positive change.
- ENFP (Campaigner) :
  Enthusiastic, creative, and highly sociable free spirits who can always find a reason to smile and explore new possibilities.
- ISTJ (Logitician) :
  Practical, factual, and extremely reliable individuals whose root focus is duty, order, and traditional structure.
- ISFJ (Defender) :
  Dedicated, warm, and highly protective caretakers who are always ready to defend and support their loved ones.
- ESTJ (Executive) :
  Excellent administrators who are unsurpassed at managing things, rules, and people efficiently.
- ESFJ (Consul) :
  Extraordinarily caring, social, and community-minded people who are always eager to help and bring harmony.
- ISTP (Virtuoso) :
  Bold, practical, and highly observant experimenters who are masters of all kinds of tools and hands-on problem-solving.
- ISFP (Adventurer) :
  Flexible, charming, and artistic individuals who are always ready to explore and experience something new.
- ESTP (Entrepreneur) :
  Smart, energetic, and highly perceptive people who truly enjoy living on the edge and taking action.
- ESFP (Entertainer) :
  Spontaneous, energetic, and enthusiastic people who love life, people, and being the center of attention.
